In English

This is a dietary supplement in tablet form containing collagen hydrolysate, biotin, and beta-carotene, supplemented with vitamin C, copper, and manganese. It is offered to support the skin and connective tissue, and is suitable for anyone wishing to add these elements to their daily diet.

  • Contains 1000 mg of collagen hydrolysate
  • Contains 500 mcg of biotin
  • Contains 40 mg of vitamin C
  • With copper and manganese
  • Lactose and gluten-free
