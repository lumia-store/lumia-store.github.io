In English

A daytime anti-ageing cream from Eucerin with SPF 30 sun protection, containing hyaluronic acid to deeply moisturise the skin and help reduce early signs of ageing. Suitable for all skin types, it can also be used as a base under make-up.

  • Suitable for all skin types
  • Contains long- and short-chain hyaluronic acid to moisturise the skin and reduce fine lines
  • Contains Glycine Saponin, which may stimulate the skin's own hyaluronic acid production
  • Contains Enoxolone, which may slow the breakdown of hyaluronic acid
  • SPF 30 sun protection helps protect against UV-related skin ageing and pigmentation spots
