In English

It is a medical product in the form of a roll-on bottle for relieving headaches and the sensation of tension. It is used on the forehead, temples, and neck, and is suitable for adults and children aged 6 years and older.

  • Helps relieve headaches and the sensation of tension
  • Works to relax muscles
  • Provides a refreshing and gentle cooling effect
  • Free from animal ingredients (vegan)
